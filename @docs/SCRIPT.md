Howdy, GT School! I'm excited to demonstrate how I've converted your pesky reconciliation errors into a solution that's useful, actionable, and simple!

On this home screen, Keystone answers a critical operations question: can we trust the data in front of us? The Trust Posture card shows whether source snapshots, invariant checks, and dashboard figures are complete and reconciled. Missing evidence is surfaced—not mistaken for a clean result.

The summary makes the reconciliation run actionable: active conflicts, evidence coverage, source status, pending proposals, spend against the daily cap, and reversible Keystone-only auto-applies.

The source cards show CRM, app database, and payments snapshots, with accepted and rejected records and generation. We can confirm freshness before investigating.

Next, a reviewer can open a conflict, compare exact source values and lineage, and understand why a proposal was suggested. Approve, reject, or hold it; every decision is captured in Keystone's audit trail.

Most importantly, Keystone never writes back to source systems. This home screen takes us from detection, to explanation, to safe human action.
